## H2 — do all non-degenerate e give 2n (or 2n+1) falls?
Under the ORIGINAL `degenerate_class(n,e,kmax=4)` rule the answer is **no**, in both directions:

## The calibrated rule [CONJ, VERIFIED on the table above]

## Cubics outside the quadratic ideal

`outside = 0` in 55 of 60 runs — the D=4 Macaulay matrix exhausts the cubic part of I(V_e), so there are no cubic generators beyond <P1,P2,P3>.

The exceptions are exactly the runs with **e = 2 itself** (not its inverse):

| n | e | outside | = |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 11 | 2 | 22 | 2n |
| 13 | 2 | 26 | 2n |
| 15 | 2 | 30 | 2n |
| 17 | 2 | 34 | 2n |
| 19 | 2 | 38 | 2n |

i.e. for e = 2 there are 2n cubics in the vanishing ideal that D=4 does not reach, while for e = 2^-1 (same fall count 7n) there are none. `falls_D4` is symmetric under e <-> e^-1 but `outside` is **not**. [OPEN] why.

## Consequence for `aim3.structures.degenerate_class`

The kmax=4 rule is both too loose and too tight (see H2 above), so it was replaced by the class rule of this file; the old behaviour is still reachable via `degenerate_class(n, e, legacy_kmax=4)`. Reason for the change is recorded in `experiments/E03_syzygy_and_degree_fall/README.md`.
